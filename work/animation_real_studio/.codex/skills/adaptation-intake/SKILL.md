# Adaptation Intake

Read `../../../docs/01-prd.md` before changing an intake contract.

1. Extract the scene's event, emotional turn, roles, relationship, setting, visual direction, dialogue intent, language, and requested outcome.
2. Preserve the user's emotional intent, but replace named copyrighted characters, unique lines, logos, and shot-for-shot requests with role-based original descriptors.
3. Require `source relationship`, rights acknowledgment, and consent before submission. Treat a claim of ownership or licence as `needs-review`, never as permission.
4. Ask only for fields that are missing or internally contradictory. Do not ask for anime clips, stills, music, real-person photos, or voice samples in the MVP.
5. Return a concise structured brief with `missing_fields`, `rights_notes`, and an `originalization_note` for the next gate.

Use a neutral rewrite such as “an anxious young courier confronts a rival at a rain-soaked station” rather than retaining a protected character name or signature world detail.
